# name-your-stops

- Read the active workflow’s cap and protected-resource rules.
- Continue authorized work after a partial result or routine failure.
- Try a distinct safe route when an attempt adds no evidence.
- At a real stop, record the residual and the smallest missing input or authority.

Owners: [loop](../../loop/SKILL.md), [believe](../../believe/SKILL.md).
Enforcement: Workflow helpers enforce their own counters. Human judgment still determines whether a new route makes progress.
